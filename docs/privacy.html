Privacy Policy / Gizlilik Politikası

Effective Date: September 2026

1. Introduction / Giriş

Welcome to Calenda ("we", "our", or "us"). Calenda is a minimalist, quiet luxury weekly planner designed to help you organize your daily schedule, build routines, and cultivate mindfulness.

We believe your personal schedule and daily habits belong solely to you. We are deeply committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding any data collected through our mobile application.

🛡️ Zero Third-Party Advertising: We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use third-party advertising SDKs or tracking networks.

2. Information We Collect / Toplanan Veriler

A. Account Information

When you register and sign in to Calenda using Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google, we receive:

  • Name: Your first name and last name (or Apple Private Relay pseudo-name) to personalize your planner.
  • Email Address: Your email address (or Apple Private Relay relay email) for unique account authentication and cloud backup.
  • User Identifier: A secure unique ID generated by the authentication provider.

B. Planner & Schedule Content

To provide core planner functionality, you create and store:

  • Weekly schedule events, routine checklists, and focus session timers.
  • Visual aesthetic preferences (color palettes, dark/light theme, widget customization).

C. On-Device Local Data & Permissions

  • Local Notifications: Local notifications are scheduled directly on your device to alert you of upcoming events and routine reminders. No notification content is sent to external advertising servers.
  • Home Screen Widgets: Data synced to home screen widgets is stored locally on your device via shared app groups.

3. How We Use Your Information / Verilerin Kullanımı

We use your information strictly for the following purposes:

  • To authenticate your identity and secure your account.
  • To synchronize your events, routines, and preferences across your devices via our secure cloud backend (Supabase).
  • To trigger local reminders and countdowns on your device.
  • To maintain app reliability and diagnose critical technical errors.

4. Data Storage, Security & Retention / Depolama ve Güvenlik

Your data is stored locally on your device using sandboxed local storage (SharedPreferences / Secure Storage) and backed up to our cloud infrastructure powered by Supabase.

  • All communications between Calenda and our cloud database are encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS/SSL encryption.
  • Row-Level Security (RLS) policies ensure that only authenticated users can view, edit, or delete their own schedule events.

5. Account & Data Deletion / Hesap ve Verileri Silme Hakkı (Apple Guideline 5.1.1)

In full compliance with Apple App Store Guideline 5.1.1(v), GDPR, and global data privacy standards, you have the full right to delete your account and all associated data at any time:

  • In-App Self Service: Open Calenda > Settings (Ayarlar) > Tap "Hesabımı ve Verilerimi Sil (Delete Account and Data)". Upon confirmation, all your cloud database records (events, routines, profile, focus history) and local cached data are permanently and irreversibly destroyed immediately.
  • By Email: You can also send a request to our official support email at calenda.support@gmail.com with the subject "Delete My Account", and we will process the complete deletion within 48 hours.

6. Third-Party Services / Üçüncü Taraf Servisler

Calenda interacts with the following trusted third-party providers strictly to deliver core app functions:

  • Apple (Sign in with Apple): Identity authentication (Apple Privacy Policy).
  • Google (Sign in with Google & Firebase Auth): Identity authentication (Google Privacy Policy).
  • Supabase: Cloud database and account session storage (Supabase Privacy Policy).

7. Children’s Privacy / Çocukların Gizliliği

Calenda is not directed to individuals under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy / Değişiklikler

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
